1. The service

Career Pilot provides tools for career exploration, learning plans, skill tracking, interview practice, resume preparation, and progress reminders. Features and availability may change as the service develops. Any access or billing terms shown in the application are the terms currently implemented; no paid plan is offered through this draft.

2. Career and AI guidance

Roadmaps, skill indicators, practice scores, vacancy summaries, and optional AI responses are informational learning aids. They may be incomplete or inaccurate and are not a promise of employment, salary, admission, certification, or hiring outcomes. Review important advice and verify job details with the employer or original listing source.

3. Your account and content

Keep your sign-in credentials private and provide accurate account information. You retain responsibility for the resume text, interview answers, coach messages, and other content you submit. Do not submit information you are not authorized to share or use the service to violate another person's rights.

4. Third-party services and listings

Authentication and saved account data use Supabase. Optional AI actions send the selected information to the configured AI provider after you request the action. Live vacancy search uses third-party listing providers; results may expire, change, or redirect to another site. Those providers' own terms and privacy practices apply to their services.

5. Availability and account controls

Some features depend on provider configuration, network access, browser support, and service limits. You can export or request deletion of account data through the controls available in the application. Deletion from active records may not immediately remove copies held in provider-managed backups.
